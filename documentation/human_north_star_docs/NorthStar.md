## What Is PoopMart?

PoopMart is a single home for everything happening in the Pop Mart collector world.

Right now, a collector has to piece together what's going on by hopping between TikTok, Instagram, Xiaohongshu, Reddit, Discord, YouTube, and resale sites — watching the same drop discussed twenty different ways, with no way to tell what's actually true or what's just noise. PoopMart pulls all of that into one feed, tells you what's actually happening, and shows you what it's worth.

It should feel like **TikTok + Reddit + StockX** built specifically for one obsession — not another shopping app, not another marketplace clone.

## How It Feels

**Opening the app feels like checking the pulse of the hobby.** An infinite, personalized feed — trending figures, new drops, price moves, leaks, hot takes — the same dopamine loop as a social feed, but every card is actually about something you collect.

**Understanding what's happening feels effortless.** Instead of watching 500 near-identical TikToks about the same drop, one card tells you the story: *"Labubu Forest Party sold out in Shanghai — resale up 40% in two days"* — with the sources underneath if you want to go deeper.

**Tracking value feels like watching a stock ticker for your shelf.** Every figure has a price, a chart, a rarity signal. Your collection has a running valuation. You know if you're sitting on something that's about to spike, or a wall of pegs nobody wants anymore.

**Being part of it feels social, not transactional.** Profiles, showcases, follows, badges, reviews, rankings — this is a place collectors hang out, not just a place they check prices.

## What Makes It Different

Most tools in this space do one thing: a price tracker, a drop calendar, a fan Discord. PoopMart is the layer that sits on top of all of them and makes sense of the noise.

- **One story, not five hundred duplicates.** AI clusters the flood of posts about the same event into a single, readable story with a sentiment read and a source count.
- **It tells you *why*, not just *what*.** Not just "price up 18%" — but "collectors are buying aggressively because production numbers appear lower than the last release."
- **It respects hands-on collector knowledge, not just data feeds.** Box-shaking, weight tells, rattle sounds — the scrappy, half-folklore techniques collectors actually use to guess what's inside a sealed box — get the same first-class treatment as price charts and drop calendars, organized and crowd-verified instead of scattered across a thousand comment threads.
- **It grows with the collector, not just the drop.** Collection management, resale analytics, and creator content sit on top of the same feed — so the app is still useful the day after a drop, not just the hour of it.

## The Experience Loop

1. **Open** — Land on a personalized feed: trending, new drops, price moves, hot takes, leaks.
2. **Understand** — Tap into a clustered story to see the AI summary, the sentiment, the sources, the price move.
3. **Go deeper** — Jump into a figure's page for its full price history, rarity, and regional availability — or into its Shake Guide to learn how collectors are telling figures apart before opening.
4. **Act** — Set a price alert, add to a wishlist, showcase a pull, follow a creator, join the discussion.
5. **Come back** — Notifications pull you back in when something you care about moves: a restock, a price drop, a livestream, a new leak.

Pop Mart collectors who are tired of stitching together what's happening from six different apps. People who want to know what's trending before it's everywhere, what their shelf is actually worth, and how to get better at the hobby — from reading a drop calendar to reading a sealed box.
